I engraved this pocket knife as a birthday present for my girlfriend. It features scrollwork I designed on one side, and her name written in her "childhood handwriting" on the other. I engraved it using the kit of engraving tools I built, including a ball vise, gravers, and a hammer and chisel handle with a quick-change design for fitting different graver profiles. This was my first experiment with background shading, and while the effect works both my tools and techniques could use some work.
