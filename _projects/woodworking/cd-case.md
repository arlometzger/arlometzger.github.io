    desctiption: "I was desparate to find a better storage solution than my big plastic bin of 400 CDs. I especially wanted to display the CDs horizontally, so the text on the spine could easily be read. I came up with the idea to make a modular bracket insert system that could turn a normal shelf into horizontal CD storage, which eventually became this: indexed horizontal slits with popsicle sticks inserted to form 360 individual CD shelves."

    description: "I used the same style of jig that's used to make finger joints to make hundreds of shallow, evenly-spaced cuts into my plywood pieces (these form the vertical columns in which CDs are 'stacked'). The jig features an aluminum indexing rail and slides across the table saw bed. The process involves making a cut, sliding the piece of wood over so that indexing rail fits into the newly made cut, making another cut, and repeating over and over and over again."
