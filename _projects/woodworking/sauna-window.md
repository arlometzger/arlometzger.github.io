I built this window for a sauna out of milled pine boards and moulding. It has a 1/16" gap to allow for expansion (hot and humid environment) and features tongue and groove joinery at the corners. It was my first time building a window, and I was surprised at the finesse required to smoothly glaze the edges. It's held up well though, and looks right at home in the sauna building.
