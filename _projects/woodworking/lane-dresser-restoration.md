I paid $80 for this beatiful 1972 Walnut Veneer Lane Dresser at a Goodwill. I used lacquer thinner to scrape away the old peeling finish, sanded it all down to 220 grit, fixed the drawer slides and other hardware, and finally finished it with some oil based polyurethane. I absolutely love the bookmatched diamond pattern in the top shelves. They don't make 'em like they used to.
