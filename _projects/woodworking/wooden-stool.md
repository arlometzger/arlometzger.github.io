    description: "I made this stool using traditional joinery techniques and materials that were already available to me. I found the Oak slab in a friend's garage, and salvaged the legs from a dining chair I thought my housemates wouldn't miss. The stool features live edge Oak steps, Rubberwood legsm mahogany wedged tenons, hand-turned/filed stretchers, and a warm walnut oil finish.

  - title: "Jigs and tools"
    description: "Getting consistent, square, and/or perfectly round results with shoddy hand tools is not easy, so some creative jig making is required. I made a simple tool to hold and position the leg pieces while I shaped them, which later evolved into a primitive lathe powered by a bow with a string. I used a wood chisel as a skew tool. This worked surprisingly well for getting nice cuts, but ultimately hand filing proved faster and prettier."
