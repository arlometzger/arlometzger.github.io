If there's one thing I hate, it's unnecessary usage of materials and space. One such offendor is plastic dental floss containers – if the floss roll is round, why is the container rectangular? Also, they just don't feel that nice to use.

I soldered this janky prototype together with some brass tubing and sheet. It uses the floss and floss cutter from a Glide floss container (which, yes, was wasted), and fits together with an indexing slot and a round central pin. It works great, and while it is far from perfect (or beautiful) it's an effective proof of concept for a more space-efficient and luxury flosser. I plan to make a future version on the lathe that closes with threads instead of press fitting together.
