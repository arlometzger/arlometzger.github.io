I bought these 90's Adenvt Legacy III speakers at an estate sale, along with a 5-track CD player, stereo receiver, and a handful of CDs. Due to their age the foam had completely disintegrated. Speaker cones can move side-to-side freely, but the foam and spider form suspension components that keep things in line and prevent "rubbing" as the cone oscillates. To ensure proper alignment, I ran a low 40-hZ tone through the speaker as I glued in a new piece of foam. I clamped everything together with clothespins, and tested them the next morning. The refoaming was a success and they sound beautiful.
