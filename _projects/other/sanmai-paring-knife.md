I made this knife by forge welding a small piece of tool steel in between two pieces of mild steel. The blade, bolster, and handle are all one piece of forged and filed steel, with recesses built in for the mahogany handle scales. I used decorative brass and steel pins to lock the handle in place.

The pattern in the blade shows the interface of the two types of steel. If the tool contained less nickel, it would etch to a darker tone than the mild steel that sandwiches it.
