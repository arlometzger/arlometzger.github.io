Hand engraving includes countless styles and techniques, ranging from scrollwork to bulino to lettering. The basic principle of cutting through metal with a tiny chisel is the same, but different chisel profiles create different effects and have specific uses. For general engraving, a 120˚ inclusive profile is often recommended, so this was the first graver I made. 

This system uses the same machined interchangeble graver holder as my engraving handles, but with an added thread on the back to connect the graver to different shaping and sharpening templates. These templates position the graver at the correct angles and distance from a sharpening stone to cut the desired profile. Polishing the cutting faces up to 1200+ grit ensures the graver will cut nice and shiny lines into the workpiece.
