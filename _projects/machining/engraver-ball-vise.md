    description: "A ball vise is one of the most useful tools for hand engraving, jewelery making, and other fine metal crafts. It can hold about any shape with an array of pins, and allows the user to work on the piece from all angles. These vises can be upwards of $1000 new, so I designed and machined this one over a semester-long machining course in my senior year of college. It rotates on two bearings, has a cross-threaded bolt to close symetrically, and has a spring-loaded locking set screw. Overall, the designing and machining took over 300 hours."

  - title: "Turning the round base"
    description: "I made the round base profile by first cutting a series of angles into a cylindrical workpiece, then filing the resulting edges down until it matched a round profile template I prepared. I then sanded and polished it to a mirror finish, so that it glides smoothly when rotating the work. In an ideal design, the radius of the base would position the workpiece to always be at the center of rotation."
