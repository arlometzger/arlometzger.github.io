"Hammer & Chisel" and "Hand Push" are the two main styles of engraving. Lacking any experience/preference, I decided to make handles for both. My designs feature a quick change graver collet, which can hold both round and square gravers with a set screw and corresponding sleeve. The only difference between the two is the wooden handle. The hand push handle features a wide surface that fits into the palm, which is pushed by hand to make cuts with the graver. The hammer & chisel handle is long and slender, and features a rounded rear end which is repeatedly tapped with a hammer to push the graver through the metal.
